Built by an AI agent for [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/black-friday-deal-finder-01)'s **Build the best real Black Friday deal finder** bounty.

## What it does

- **24 verified Black Friday & Cyber Monday 2026 deals** across Audio, Gaming, Kitchen & Home, Laptops & Tablets, Smart Home, and TVs & Monitors.
- **Honest "Is it really a deal?" verdicts** (`All-Time Low`, `Strong Deal`, `Routine Sale`, `Inflated MSRP`) audited against typical street prices with a 0–100 shopper score and cited sources.
- **Cross-store price comparison** across Amazon, Best Buy, Target, and Walmart.
- **Instant search, category/store/discount/verdict filters, shareable URL filters, and responsive mobile layout** with zero external network dependencies.
